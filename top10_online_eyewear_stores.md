## Platform Deep Dives

### 1. Zenni Optical
**Website:** zenni.com  
**Starting Price:** Frames from $6.95 (single-vision lenses included)

**Highlights:**
- One of the largest frame selections online — thousands of styles
- All-inclusive pricing: basic single-vision lenses bundled with frames
- Virtual try-on using face photo or 3D face scan
- Blokz blue-light blocking lenses available
- Ships to 40+ countries, though US-centric in pricing/experience

**Best For:** Anyone wanting the absolute lowest price without sacrificing variety.

---

### 2. EyeBuyDirect
**Website:** eyebuydirect.com  
**Starting Price:** Frames from $6 (lenses from $14 extra for single-vision)

**Highlights:**
- Frequent sitewide promotions (BOGO, 50% off sales)
- Fashion-forward collaborations and in-house designer styles
- RFLKT blue-light and SunSync photochromic lens options
- 14-day fit guarantee with easy exchanges
- Ships to 50+ countries

**Best For:** Style-conscious shoppers who want trendy frames at low prices.

---

### 3. Warby Parker
**Website:** warbyparker.com  
**Starting Price:** $95 (frames + single-vision lenses)

**Highlights:**
- Premium brand with strong design aesthetic and quality control
- Home Try-On program: receive 5 frames to test for 5 days, free of charge
- Physical retail stores across the US and Canada (in-person eye exams available)
- Buy-a-pair, give-a-pair social mission
- App-based virtual try-on and prescription check (Prescription Check app)

**Best For:** Shoppers willing to pay more for a curated, brand-trust experience with the option of in-store service.

---

### 4. GlassesUSA
**Website:** glassesusa.com  
**Starting Price:** ~$29 for house frames; branded frames from ~$69

**Highlights:**
- Carries licensed designer brands (Ray-Ban, Coach, Nike, Oakley, etc.)
- Up to 70% off retail prices on brand-name frames
- 365-day warranty and 14-day returns
- Free basic lenses on many frames
- Frequent coupon codes bring prices down significantly

**Best For:** Shoppers who want recognizable designer brands at a discount.

---

### 5. Clearly (clearly.ca / clearly.com.au)
**Website:** clearly.ca (Canada) / clearly.com.au (Australia)  
**Starting Price:** ~CAD $39 / AUD $39 (frames + single-vision lenses)

**Highlights:**
- Well-established in Canada and Australia with local pricing and shipping
- First pair free promotion available for new customers (select frames)
- Also carries contact lenses alongside glasses
- Wide frame selection including house brand and licensed styles
- Useful for AU/CA customers who prefer local returns/support

**Best For:** Canadian and Australian shoppers looking for a local-market-friendly platform.

---

### 6. Glasses Direct
**Website:** glassesdirect.co.uk  
**Starting Price:** ~£25 (frames + single-vision lenses)

**Highlights:**
- UK's largest online glasses retailer
- Home trial program: try up to 4 pairs for 7 days free
- Accepts NHS vouchers (UK patients can use vision care benefits)
- Price Beat Guarantee on comparable prescriptions
- Strong focus on UK-based customer service and returns

**Best For:** UK residents, especially those with NHS optical vouchers.

### 7. GlassesShop
**Website:** glassesshop.com

**Highlights:**

- Bold, fashion-forward styles popular with younger shoppers
- First pair free promotion for new customers (pay only shipping)
- Virtual try-on available
- Occasional deep-discount promotions

### 9. Coastal

### 10. Brille24
**Website:** brille24.de  
**Starting Price:** ~€29 (frames + single-vision lenses)

**Highlights:**
- Germany's leading online glasses retailer, well-established in the EU market
- Ships across Germany and most of Europe
- Home trial available (try before you buy)
- Carries designer brand frames as well as own-brand options
- German-language interface; strong EU customer protections and returns

**Best For:** European (especially German-speaking) shoppers who want a trusted local platform.

## Key Factors to Consider When Choosing

| Factor | What to Look For |
|---|---|
| **Prescription complexity** | High prescriptions (strong SPH, CYL, or prism) may not be well-supported by ultra-budget sites. Check each platform's rx limits. |
| **Lens upgrades** | Base price often covers single-vision only. Progressive, photochromic, and anti-reflective coatings add cost. |
| **Try-before-you-buy** | Warby Parker, Glasses Direct, Coastal, and Clearly offer home trial programs. |
| **Return policy** | Look for at least 14–30 days. Some sites offer remakes if the prescription feels off. |
| **Shipping destination** | Not all platforms ship globally. Confirm your region before ordering. |
| **Customer support** | Budget platforms may rely heavily on email; mid-tier and premium platforms typically offer phone/chat. |
