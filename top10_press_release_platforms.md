## Detailed Platform Profiles

---

### 1. PR Newswire
**Website:** https://www.prnewswire.com/
**Starting Price:** ~$350 per release (national US); enterprise plans vary widely

**Overview:**
One of the oldest and most recognized names in press release distribution, PR Newswire (owned by Cision) has been the industry standard for over 70 years. It boasts one of the largest media networks in the world, reaching 500,000+ media outlets, newsrooms, and influencers globally.

**Best For:**
- Large enterprises and Fortune 500 companies
- Major product launches, IPOs, and crisis communications
- Brands that prioritize credibility and name recognition

**Strengths:**
- Widest distribution network in the industry
- 24/7 editorial support with expert editors
- AI-powered press release scoring and optimization
- Strong journalist and newsroom relationships

**Limitations:**
- Expensive — costs can reach $7,000–$10,000+ annually with add-ons
- Less cost-effective for small businesses or single releases
- Distribution can be broad rather than targeted

---

### 2. Business Wire
**Website:** https://www.businesswire.com/
**Starting Price:** ~$400 per release (US national circuit)

**Overview:**
A Berkshire Hathaway company, Business Wire is a direct competitor to PR Newswire and is widely used by publicly traded companies and large corporations. It has a particularly strong reputation in financial and investor relations communications.

**Best For:**
- Publicly traded companies and investor relations teams
- Financial disclosures, earnings announcements, M&A news
- Formal corporate press releases requiring regulatory compliance

**Strengths:**
- Deep penetration in financial media (Bloomberg, Reuters, AP)
- Strong compliance infrastructure for SEC and regulatory filings
- Global distribution in 160+ countries
- Trusted by Wall Street and institutional investors

**Limitations:**
- High pricing, similar to PR Newswire
- Primarily suited for corporate and financial announcements
- Interface and workflow can feel dated

---

### 3. GlobeNewswire
**Website:** https://www.globenewswire.com/
**Starting Price:** Custom pricing (contact for quote)

**Overview:**
Owned by Notified (formerly part of Nasdaq), GlobeNewswire specializes in financial and investor-focused press releases. It supports multilingual distribution and has strong penetration in European and North American financial markets.

**Best For:**
- Publicly listed companies across North America and Europe
- Investor relations, ESG disclosures, and earnings reports
- Organizations needing multilingual global distribution

**Strengths:**
- Strong integration with financial data terminals
- Multilingual publishing across major world markets
- Trusted by institutional investors and analysts
- Detailed analytics and reporting tools

**Limitations:**
- Pricing is not transparent; requires direct contact for quotes
- Less suited for non-financial or consumer-facing announcements
- Smaller total media network than PR Newswire

---

### 4. eReleases
**Website:** https://www.ereleases.com/
**Starting Price:** ~$399 per release

**Overview:**
eReleases bridges the gap between SMBs and enterprise-level distribution. It provides access to the PR Newswire wire network at a lower cost, bundled with a journalist database and editorial review services — making it one of the best value options for serious media outreach.

**Best For:**
- Small to mid-sized businesses wanting premium wire coverage
- Companies seeking editorial guidance and writing support
- Brands targeting specific journalists or industry verticals

**Strengths:**
- Access to PR Newswire's distribution network without full enterprise pricing
- Journalist database included
- Personal editorial review on each release
- Transparent, flat-rate pricing

**Limitations:**
- More expensive than purely self-service platforms
- No free tier or low-cost trial available
- Turnaround time slightly slower due to editorial process

---

### 5. Newswire
**Website:** https://www.newswire.com/
**Starting Price:** ~$149 per release (basic); subscription plans from ~$349/month

**Overview:**
Newswire offers a modern, media-forward distribution platform with broad syndication channels across digital, broadcast, and print outlets. It also offers a media contact database and earned media tools suited for PR teams managing ongoing campaigns.

**Best For:**
- Growing businesses running regular PR campaigns
- PR agencies managing multiple clients
- Brands combining press releases with social and digital media outreach

**Strengths:**
- Wide distribution across web, broadcast, and print
- Built-in media contact database for targeted pitching
- Campaign management and earned media monitoring tools
- Scalable subscription pricing for high-volume users

**Limitations:**
- Premium features require higher-tier plans
- Less name recognition among tier-1 journalists compared to PR Newswire
- Analytics can vary in depth depending on plan level

### 6. PRWeb
**Website:** https://www.prweb.com/
**Starting Price:** ~$99 per release

**Overview:**
Owned by Cision (same parent as PR Newswire), PRWeb targets smaller businesses and startups seeking affordable distribution with strong SEO benefits. It syndicates releases to thousands of online news sites and search engines, making it effective for digital visibility.

**Best For:**
- Startups and small businesses on a limited budget
- Companies prioritizing online search visibility and SEO backlinks
- Simple product announcements, event news, or brand updates

**Strengths:**
- Very affordable entry price
- Strong SEO optimization and Google News indexing
- Easy, self-service submission workflow
- Part of the trusted Cision network

**Limitations:**
- Does not reach major print or broadcast newsrooms
- Lower perceived credibility with top-tier journalists
- Limited targeting options compared to premium platforms

### 7. 24-7 Press Release
**Website:** https://www.24-7pressrelease.com/
**Starting Price:** ~$19 per release (Visibility Boost plan)

**Overview:**
One of the most budget-friendly paid distribution services available, 24-7 Press Release is designed for small businesses and solo entrepreneurs who need quick, easy distribution without a large investment. It offers multiple pricing tiers and same-day publication.

**Strengths:**

**Limitations:**

EIN Presswire offers affordable global distribution with a focus on multi-industry targeting and social media amplification. It's popular with startups, independent projects, and international brands looking for cost-effective reach across niche verticals.

**Best For:**

**Strengths:**

- Industry-specific targeting across 700+ channels
- Distribution to USA TODAY Network news sites
- Social media and mobile delivery included

**Limitations:**

- Less editorial support than premium services
- Coverage of major print outlets is limited

### 9. PR.com
**Website:** https://www.pr.com/
**Starting Price:** Free (basic listing); paid plans from ~$30/release

**Overview:**
PR.com is a hybrid directory and distribution platform that combines a business directory with press release hosting. It is particularly useful for small businesses and entrepreneurs who want basic online visibility without spending on distribution.

**Best For:**
- Solo entrepreneurs, small local businesses, and nonprofits
- Companies wanting free online press release hosting
- Brands seeking a business directory listing alongside PR visibility

**Strengths:**
- Free option available with no expiration
- Business profile and directory listing included
- Simple submission process
- Good for SEO link building on a zero budget

**Limitations:**
- Free plan offers very limited active distribution
- Paid plans still have limited reach compared to wire services
- Not appropriate for major announcements or high-stakes news

## Key Takeaways

- **Enterprise brands** should prioritize **PR Newswire** or **Business Wire** for credibility and reach.
- **SMBs and startups** get the best ROI from **eReleases**, **EIN Presswire**, or **PRWeb**.
- **SEO-focused campaigns** benefit most from **PRWeb** and **Newswire**.
- **Zero-budget distribution** is possible with **PRFREE** and **PR.com**, though reach is limited.
- **Mid-budget ($50–150)** SMBs may also consider **NewswireBiz** for quick-turn wire exposure.
- **Lean teams** wanting low-cost online amplification can evaluate **PressReleaseWire** as a budget option.
- For high-volume campaigns, consider subscription plans on **Newswire** or bundle pricing on **EIN Presswire**.
