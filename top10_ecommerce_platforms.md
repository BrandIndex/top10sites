## Detailed Platform Profiles

### 1. Shopify
**Website:** https://www.shopify.com/
**Starting Price:** ~$29/month (Basic plan)

**Overview:**
Shopify is the most successful hosted e-commerce platform in the world, powering over 4 million stores across 175 countries. From solo print-on-demand sellers to billion-dollar DTC brands, Shopify scales across every stage of business growth. Its App Store (8,000+ apps) and built-in payment processing make it a complete commerce operating system.

**Best For:**
- Any size online store — solo to enterprise
- Brands that sell across multiple channels (web, social, POS, marketplaces)
- Dropshipping and print-on-demand operations

**Strengths:**
- Best multi-channel selling (web, Instagram, TikTok, Amazon, POS)
- 8,000+ apps in the Shopify App Store
- Built-in Shopify Payments (accept credit cards without a third-party gateway)
- Shopify Shipping with discounted carrier rates
- Shopify Capital for revenue-based funding

**Limitations:**
- 2% transaction fee on non-Shopify Payments gateways
- Themes can be expensive ($200+)
- Heavy app reliance for advanced features increases monthly costs

### 2. WooCommerce
**Website:** https://woocommerce.com/
**Starting Price:** Free plugin (WordPress hosting required, typically $5–$30/month)

**Overview:**
WooCommerce is the most widely used e-commerce platform in the world by install count, powering 27%+ of all online stores. As a free WordPress plugin, it transforms any WordPress site into a full-featured store. Its flexibility and open-source nature make it infinitely customizable — but require more technical involvement.

**Best For:**
- WordPress users adding e-commerce to an existing site
- Developers building custom e-commerce solutions
- Stores that need complete control over code and data

**Strengths:**
- Free core plugin (pay only for hosting, theme, and extensions)
- 1,000+ extensions (payment gateways, shipping, subscriptions, memberships)
- Full data ownership — your server, your database
- Largest developer ecosystem of any e-commerce platform
- Ideal for complex products (configurable products, subscriptions, bookings)

**Limitations:**
- Requires WordPress and web hosting management
- Total cost of ownership can exceed Shopify when adding paid extensions
- Performance optimization requires technical knowledge

### 3. BigCommerce
**Website:** https://www.bigcommerce.com/
**Starting Price:** ~$29/month (Standard plan)

**Overview:**
BigCommerce is the best Shopify alternative for high-volume and mid-market stores. Unlike Shopify, BigCommerce charges no transaction fees on any payment gateway. It natively supports complex B2B features (price lists, customer groups, purchase orders) without apps.

**Best For:**
- High-volume stores that want to avoid Shopify's transaction fees
- B2B and wholesale businesses
- Enterprise retailers on Shopify Plus-level needs at lower cost

**Strengths:**
- No transaction fees on any payment gateway (unique vs Shopify)
- Built-in B2B features without apps (price lists, customer groups, quoting)
- Multi-storefront on Enterprise plan
- Headless commerce architecture supported natively
- Generous built-in features (no app needed for product reviews, gift cards, etc.)

**Limitations:**
- Annual sales caps on lower plans force upgrades (Standard: $50K/year)
- Smaller app ecosystem than Shopify
- Interface slightly less polished than Shopify

### 4. Wix eCommerce
**Website:** https://www.wix.com/ecommerce/
**Starting Price:** Free plan (limited) / ~$17/month (Light plan with e-commerce)

**Overview:**
Wix eCommerce brings Shopify-like store functionality to Wix's already-popular website builder. It's the best choice for creative small businesses and service providers who need a beautiful website first and a store second — without managing two separate platforms.

**Best For:**
- Small creative businesses (boutiques, artisans, designers)
- Service businesses with a small product catalog
- Beginners who want website + store in the simplest package

**Strengths:**
- Wix's renowned drag-and-drop editor for beautiful storefronts
- 900+ Wix App Market integrations
- Multiple payment options including Wix Payments, PayPal, Stripe
- Wix Bookings for service-based businesses
- No transaction fees on Wix Payments

**Limitations:**
- Cannot migrate a Wix site to another platform
- Not suitable for large product catalogs (100+)
- Less powerful inventory and order management than Shopify

### 5. Squarespace Commerce
**Website:** https://www.squarespace.com/ecommerce/
**Starting Price:** ~$23/month (Business plan, annual)

**Overview:**
Squarespace Commerce is the best-looking e-commerce platform for design-obsessed brands. Its award-winning templates, integrated blogging, and portfolio tools make it the top choice for photographers, clothing brands, and lifestyle businesses where aesthetics drive purchasing decisions.

**Best For:**
- Fashion, jewelry, art, and lifestyle brands where aesthetics matter
- Photographers and creatives selling prints or services
- Small stores that prioritize visual brand identity over complex features

**Strengths:**
- Best-in-class visual design and templates in the market
- No transaction fees on Commerce plans
- Integrated email marketing (Squarespace Email Campaigns)
- Subscription selling and member areas built in
- Instagram Shopping integration

**Limitations:**
- Limited app marketplace compared to Shopify
- Less powerful inventory management for large catalogs
- Not cost-effective for high-volume stores

### 6. Ecwid by Lightspeed
**Website:** https://www.ecwid.com/
**Starting Price:** Free (up to 5 products) / ~$19/month (Venture)

**Overview:**
Ecwid is unique: instead of replacing your existing website, it adds a store to it. Paste a code snippet into any existing website — WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, or a custom site — and you have a fully functional online store. It's the only platform designed to layer onto existing web presences.

**Best For:**
- Businesses with existing websites that want to add a store
- Sellers on multiple platforms (add the same store to a website AND a Facebook page AND Instagram)
- Small businesses with existing web presence (WordPress blogs, etc.)

**Strengths:**
- Embeds into any existing website (WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, custom)
- Sell on Facebook, Instagram, Google Shopping, TikTok from one back end
- Free plan available (up to 5 products)
- Automatic tax calculation in supported regions
- Mobile app for store management

**Limitations:**
- Not designed as a standalone website builder
- Advanced features (subscriptions, digital goods) require paid plans
- Smaller feature set than Shopify for standalone stores

### 7. PrestaShop
**Website:** https://www.prestashop.com/
**Starting Price:** Free (self-hosted open-source)

**Overview:**
PrestaShop is the most popular open-source e-commerce platform in Europe. It's available in two versions: self-hosted (free, open-source) and PrestaShop Hosted (cloud, paid). With strong multi-language, multi-currency, and EU tax compliance built in, it's the natural choice for European retailers.

**Best For:**
- European online retailers needing multi-language and multi-currency support
- Mid-market stores that want open-source flexibility
- Developers building custom European e-commerce solutions

**Strengths:**
- Strong built-in multi-language and multi-currency support
- EU tax compliance (VAT, GDPR) built in
- 7,000+ modules and themes in the marketplace
- Free self-hosted version with no licensing fees
- Large European developer and agency community

**Limitations:**
- Self-hosted version requires technical management
- Module quality and compatibility can vary
- Support for self-hosted version is community-based

### 8. OpenCart
**Website:** https://www.opencart.com/
**Starting Price:** Free (open-source, self-hosted)

**Overview:**
OpenCart is a lightweight, developer-friendly open-source e-commerce platform. Its modular architecture and large extension marketplace (13,000+ extensions) make it popular for developers who want a fully customizable starting point without the WordPress dependency of WooCommerce.

**Best For:**
- Developers who want an open-source platform without WordPress
- Technical merchants who want full server control
- Stores needing budget open-source e-commerce

**Strengths:**
- Completely free and open-source
- 13,000+ modules and themes
- Multi-store management from one admin panel
- Lightweight — performs well on modest hosting
- No per-transaction fees

**Limitations:**
- Requires significant developer involvement for customization and updates
- Smaller community and support ecosystem than WooCommerce
- Not as turnkey as hosted platforms for non-technical users

---

### 9. Shift4Shop (formerly 3dcart)
**Website:** https://www.shift4shop.com/
**Starting Price:** Free (US merchants using Shift4 Payments)

**Overview:**
Shift4Shop offers a full-featured e-commerce platform completely free — for US businesses that process payments through Shift4 Payments. For merchants willing to use its payment processor, it's the best-value hosted e-commerce platform available, with features rivaling Shopify's mid-tier.

**Best For:**
- US-based merchants happy to use Shift4 Payments
- Small businesses who want a feature-rich hosted store at zero cost
- Sellers who want a Shopify-like experience for free

**Strengths:**
- Completely free for US merchants on Shift4 Payments
- Unlimited products on all plans
- Built-in SEO tools, blog, reviews, loyalty points
- 100+ free themes
- No transaction fees (with Shift4 Payments)

**Limitations:**
- Free plan tied to Shift4 Payments (not available outside the US)
- Monthly fee required for non-US merchants or other payment gateways
- Smaller community than Shopify or WooCommerce

---

### 10. Volusion
**Website:** https://www.volusion.com/
**Starting Price:** ~$35/month (Personal plan, annual)

**Overview:**
Volusion is one of the original hosted e-commerce platforms (founded 1999), known for its built-in analytics and reporting capabilities. It's a solid option for data-driven small business owners who want deep sales reporting without a separate analytics tool.

**Best For:**
- Small business owners focused on sales analytics and reporting
- Merchants who want a stable, long-established hosted platform
- Stores that prioritize data over design

**Strengths:**
- Detailed built-in analytics (sales reports, inventory reports, marketing reports)
- No transaction fees on any plan
- In-house site designer service available
- Subscription billing built in

**Limitations:**
- Annual sales caps on lower plans force upgrades
- Design and template quality trails Shopify and Squarespace
- Smaller app ecosystem than Shopify or WooCommerce
