## Detailed Platform Profiles

---

### 1. Mailchimp
**Website:** https://mailchimp.com/
**Starting Price:** Free (up to 500 contacts) / ~$13/month (Essentials)

**Overview:**
Mailchimp is the most recognized email marketing brand in the world. Its free tier, intuitive drag-and-drop builder, and all-in-one marketing tools (landing pages, social ads, basic CRM) make it the default starting point for small businesses and first-time email marketers.

**Best For:**
- Beginners launching their first email newsletter
- Small businesses that want one tool for email + basic marketing
- Non-profits and freelancers on a tight budget

**Strengths:**
- Free tier includes 500 contacts and 1,000 sends/month
- Drag-and-drop email builder with 100+ templates
- Pre-built Customer Journey automation maps
- Built-in landing pages, social ads, and basic CRM

**Limitations:**
- Free tier is limited and doesn't include automation
- Contact-based pricing gets expensive quickly as the list grows
- Deliverability is average compared to specialized platforms

### 2. Klaviyo
**Website:** https://www.klaviyo.com/
**Starting Price:** Free (up to 250 contacts) / ~$20/month (Email, 251–500 contacts)

**Overview:**
Klaviyo is the gold standard email marketing platform for e-commerce. Its deep Shopify integration, revenue attribution (see exactly how much each email earns), and behavioral triggers (abandoned cart, browse abandonment, post-purchase) make it indispensable for DTC brands.

**Best For:**
- Shopify, WooCommerce, and BigCommerce store owners
- DTC brands with product catalogs who want granular automation
- E-commerce teams focused on revenue attribution and ROI

**Strengths:**
- Best Shopify integration in the industry (real-time sync)
- Revenue attribution per email and flow
- Predictive analytics (CLV, churn risk, next order date)
- SMS + email marketing from the same platform

**Limitations:**
- Expensive for large contact lists compared to Brevo or ActiveCampaign
- Steeper learning curve for non-e-commerce use cases

---

### 3. ActiveCampaign
**Website:** https://www.activecampaign.com/
**Starting Price:** ~$15/month (Starter, annual billing, up to 1,000 contacts)

**Overview:**
ActiveCampaign combines email marketing with a powerful CRM and sales automation layer. Its visual automation builder is the most capable in the market — with conditional branching, lead scoring, site tracking, and SMS — making it the top pick for sophisticated lifecycle marketing.

**Best For:**
- Marketing teams that need complex automation sequences
- B2B companies that want CRM + email in one platform
- Agencies managing multi-step customer journeys

**Strengths:**
- Most powerful visual automation builder in the market
- Built-in CRM with lead scoring and pipeline management
- Site tracking and event-based triggers
- 870+ integrations

**Limitations:**
- Overwhelming for simple newsletter use cases
- Pricing scales steeply with contact list size

---

### 4. ConvertKit
**Website:** https://convertkit.com/
**Starting Price:** Free (up to 10,000 subscribers) / ~$9/month (Creator)

**Overview:**
ConvertKit is purpose-built for individual creators — bloggers, podcasters, YouTubers, course sellers, and newsletter writers. Its subscriber-centric model (tag-based, not list-based) makes it easy to send the right content to the right audience segments.

**Best For:**
- Individual creators, bloggers, and online course sellers
- Newsletter writers who want to monetize their audience
- Podcasters and YouTubers building email lists

**Strengths:**
- Free tier up to 10,000 subscribers (most generous in this list)
- Creator-friendly features: paid newsletters, digital product sales
- Tag-based subscriber management (simpler than list-based systems)
- ConvertKit Commerce for selling digital products

**Limitations:**
- Limited design templates (intentionally text-focused)
- E-commerce/Shopify integration not as deep as Klaviyo

---

### 5. Brevo (formerly Sendinblue)
**Website:** https://www.brevo.com/
**Starting Price:** Free (300 emails/day) / ~$8/month (Starter, 20,000 emails/mo)

**Overview:**
Brevo (rebranded from Sendinblue in 2023) offers the best value proposition in email marketing — pricing by emails sent, not contacts stored. This makes it dramatically more affordable for large lists with infrequent sends, and its free tier allows 300 emails per day with unlimited contacts.

**Best For:**
- Businesses with large contact lists but moderate send frequency
- Transactional email and SMS alongside marketing campaigns
- Small businesses wanting automation without high cost

**Strengths:**
- Priced by emails sent (not contacts) — great for large, infrequently mailed lists
- Unlimited contacts on all plans
- Transactional email (SMTP) included on all plans
- SMS, WhatsApp, and live chat alongside email

**Limitations:**
- Brevo branding on emails in free tier
- Deliverability reputation trails Klaviyo and ActiveCampaign

### 6. Constant Contact
**Website:** https://www.constantcontact.com/
**Starting Price:** ~$12/month (Lite, up to 500 contacts)

**Overview:**
Constant Contact is one of the oldest email marketing services and has built a reputation for excellent customer support and straightforward usability. It's particularly popular with non-profits, local businesses, and event organizers.

**Best For:**
- Non-profits and community organizations
- Local small businesses with simple email needs
- Event marketing (built-in RSVP and event management tools)

**Strengths:**
- Phone and live chat support included on all plans
- Event marketing tools (RSVPs, ticketing, event reminders)
- Drag-and-drop builder with 200+ templates
- Social media posting and ads integration

**Limitations:**
- More expensive per contact than Brevo or ConvertKit
- Automation is basic compared to ActiveCampaign or Klaviyo

### 7. GetResponse
**Website:** https://www.getresponse.com/
**Starting Price:** Free (up to 500 contacts) / ~$13/month (Email Marketing, 1,000 contacts)

**Overview:**
GetResponse differentiates itself by bundling webinar hosting with email marketing. You can host a webinar, automatically add attendees to an email sequence, and nurture them through a sales funnel — all within one platform.

**Best For:**
- Businesses that run webinars as a lead generation channel
- Course creators combining email + live events
- E-commerce teams needing a full funnel platform

**Strengths:**
- Built-in webinar hosting (up to 1,000 attendees on higher plans)
- Conversion funnels builder (opt-in → email → landing page → checkout)
- AI-powered email generator and subject line optimizer
- Paid newsletter and premium email features

**Limitations:**
- Webinar features require higher-tier plans
- Interface can feel dated compared to Klaviyo or ConvertKit

---

### 8. AWeber
**Website:** https://www.aweber.com/
**Starting Price:** Free (up to 500 subscribers) / ~$12.50/month (Lite, annual)

**Overview:**
AWeber is one of the original email marketing platforms (founded 1998) and has served as the backbone for newsletters and online businesses for nearly three decades. Its reliability, deliverability, and straightforward interface continue to serve established newsletter businesses well.

**Best For:**
- Long-established newsletter publishers who depend on deliverability
- Small businesses and bloggers with simple needs
- Users who want proven, no-surprises reliability

**Strengths:**
- Strong deliverability track record
- 700+ email templates
- Free migration service from other platforms
- AMP for Email support (interactive elements in emails)

**Limitations:**
- Interface and automation tools feel behind modern competitors
- Pricing is not competitive relative to Brevo or ConvertKit for value

---

### 9. Drip
**Website:** https://www.drip.com/
**Starting Price:** ~$39/month (up to 2,500 contacts)

**Overview:**
Drip is a dedicated e-commerce CRM and email platform designed specifically for DTC brands. Its revenue attribution, behavioral segmentation (by purchase history, browse behavior, and LTV), and deep WooCommerce/Shopify integrations make it a compelling Klaviyo alternative.

**Best For:**
- WooCommerce store owners who find Klaviyo expensive
- DTC brands focused on post-purchase and retention marketing
- E-commerce teams that want a clean, email-focused CRM

**Strengths:**
- E-commerce-specific automation (post-purchase, win-back, VIP)
- Revenue attribution per campaign and sequence
- Visual workflow builder with e-commerce event triggers
- Integrates natively with WooCommerce, Shopify, and Magento

**Limitations:**
- No free tier
- More expensive entry point than Brevo or Mailchimp
- Smaller company than Klaviyo with fewer native integrations

---

### 10. Campaign Monitor
**Website:** https://www.campaignmonitor.com/
**Starting Price:** ~$9/month (Basic, up to 2,500 emails/month)

**Overview:**
Campaign Monitor is the email platform of choice for design-conscious agencies and marketing teams. Its template builder is among the most beautiful and flexible available, and its client management features make it ideal for agencies sending on behalf of multiple brands.

**Best For:**
- Design agencies managing email for multiple clients
- Brands where visual quality of emails is a primary concern
- Marketing teams with high design standards

**Strengths:**
- Best-in-class drag-and-drop email template designer
- Agency-friendly: client accounts and sub-accounts
- Link Review feature flags broken links before sending
- Advanced segmentation on higher-tier plans

**Limitations:**
- More expensive per send than Brevo for infrequent senders
- E-commerce features not as deep as Klaviyo or Drip
