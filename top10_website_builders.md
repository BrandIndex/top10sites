## Detailed Builder Profiles

---

### 1. Wix
**Website:** https://www.wix.com/
**Starting Price:** Free (with Wix branding) / ~$17/month (Light plan)

**Overview:**
Wix is the most popular drag-and-drop website builder in the world, used by over 250 million people. Its combination of total design freedom, a massive app market, and AI-powered tools (Wix ADI) make it the most versatile option for non-coders.

**Best For:**
- Creative professionals who want pixel-perfect control
- Small businesses needing a feature-rich website
- Users who want to start free and upgrade when ready

**Strengths:**
- Truly free-form drag-and-drop editor (most flexible in the market)
- 800+ designer-made templates
- Wix App Market with 300+ third-party integrations
- Wix AI builder creates a full site in minutes from prompts

**Limitations:**
- Wix sites cannot be migrated to other platforms easily
- Free plan shows Wix ads and uses a Wix subdomain

### 2. Squarespace
**Website:** https://www.squarespace.com/
**Starting Price:** ~$16/month (Personal plan, annual billing)

**Overview:**
Squarespace is the design leader among website builders. Its templates are crafted by world-class designers, and every element — typography, color, spacing — is carefully constrained to ensure a polished result. It's the top choice for photographers, artists, and agencies.

**Best For:**
- Photographers, artists, designers, and creatives
- Portfolio websites and personal brands
- Small stores that prioritize aesthetics

**Strengths:**
- Best-in-class template designs in the industry
- Fluid Engine drag-and-drop editor (launched 2022)
- Built-in email marketing (Squarespace Email Campaigns)
- Strong blogging and podcasting tools

**Limitations:**
- Less flexible than Wix for pixel-precise customization
- App marketplace is smaller than Wix or WordPress

---

### 3. Shopify
**Website:** https://www.shopify.com/
**Starting Price:** ~$29/month (Basic plan)

**Overview:**
Shopify is the undisputed king of e-commerce website builders. It powers over 4 million stores worldwide and handles everything from product listings to payments, shipping, and inventory — at scale. If your primary goal is selling products, Shopify is the benchmark.

**Best For:**
- Online stores of any size (from solo sellers to enterprise)
- Businesses that sell across multiple channels (web, social, POS)
- Dropshipping operations

**Strengths:**
- Best-in-class e-commerce tooling (inventory, payments, shipping)
- 8,000+ apps in the Shopify App Store
- Built-in Shopify Payments (no third-party gateway needed)
- Supports selling on Instagram, TikTok, Amazon, and in-person POS

**Limitations:**
- 2% transaction fee if not using Shopify Payments
- Not ideal for content-heavy sites (blogging is secondary)

---

### 4. Webflow
**Website:** https://webflow.com/
**Starting Price:** Free (2 pages) / ~$14/month (Basic plan)

**Overview:**
Webflow is a visual web design tool that outputs clean, production-ready HTML/CSS/JS. It sits between a traditional website builder and a coding environment — giving designers the power of custom code without actually writing it.

**Best For:**
- Web designers who want more control than Wix/Squarespace
- Agencies building custom client sites
- Developers who want a CMS without backend work

**Strengths:**
- Outputs clean, custom HTML/CSS (not locked into proprietary markup)
- Powerful CMS for content-driven sites
- Animation and interaction tools beyond any competitor
- Hosting included with fast Fastly CDN

**Limitations:**
- Steep learning curve for non-designers
- Higher price point for agency-tier features

---

### 5. WordPress.com
**Website:** https://wordpress.com/
**Starting Price:** Free / ~$4/month (Personal plan, annual)

**Overview:**
WordPress.com (the hosted version of WordPress) offers the world's most popular CMS in a managed, no-code environment. It's the top choice for bloggers and publishers who want powerful content management without managing a server.

**Best For:**
- Bloggers and content publishers
- News sites and editorial publications
- Users who want to grow into self-hosted WordPress later

**Strengths:**
- Powered by WordPress — most plugin and theme ecosystem in the world
- SEO tools built in (Jetpack)
- Scales from hobby blog to high-traffic media site
- Free tier available with WordPress subdomain

**Limitations:**
- Plugin installation requires Business plan or higher
- Ecommerce features trail Shopify significantly

---

### 6. Weebly
**Website:** https://www.weebly.com/
**Starting Price:** Free / ~$10/month (Personal plan, annual)

**Overview:**
Weebly (owned by Square) is the simplest drag-and-drop builder for users who need a clean, functional website fast. It won't win design awards, but it gets the job done for basic small business sites and integrates natively with Square's payment ecosystem.

**Best For:**
- Small local businesses (restaurants, salons, service providers)
- Users who want the simplest possible setup
- Businesses already using Square for payments

**Strengths:**
- Simplest setup in the market — live in under an hour
- Native integration with Square POS and payments
- Free plan available (with Weebly subdomain)
- App Center for basic third-party integrations

**Limitations:**
- Design flexibility significantly limited compared to Wix or Squarespace
- Templates feel outdated relative to modern builders

### 7. GoDaddy Website Builder
**Website:** https://www.godaddy.com/websites/website-builder
**Starting Price:** ~$9.99/month (Basic plan, annual)

**Overview:**
GoDaddy's AI-powered Website Builder creates a complete site — including content — from a few prompts in minutes. It's built for speed over customization, making it ideal for businesses that just need a basic, professional online presence quickly.

**Best For:**
- Small businesses that need a site up in under an hour
- GoDaddy domain customers who want everything in one place
- Users who don't care about design detail

**Strengths:**
- AI site builder creates pages and content automatically
- Integrated with GoDaddy's domain and email ecosystem
- Built-in SEO checklist and marketing tools
- 24/7 support

**Limitations:**
- Very limited design customization
- Not suitable for complex or e-commerce sites

### 8. Jimdo
**Website:** https://www.jimdo.com/
**Starting Price:** Free / ~$9/month (Start plan, annual)

**Overview:**
Jimdo's Dolphin AI builder is a unique proposition — answer a few questions, and an AI generates your complete website in minutes, including images, text, and layout. For businesses that need "something professional, fast," it delivers.

**Best For:**
- Non-technical business owners who want speed
- Local businesses and freelancers needing a simple site
- Users who want an AI to do all the heavy lifting

**Strengths:**
- Dolphin AI creates a personalized site in under 3 minutes
- GDPR-compliant by default (Germany-based company)
- Simple, clean interface with no clutter
- Free plan available

**Limitations:**
- Design flexibility is the most limited of all builders here
- Template variety is narrower than Wix or Squarespace

### 9. Strikingly
**Website:** https://www.strikingly.com/
**Starting Price:** Free / ~$8/month (Limited plan, annual)

**Overview:**
Strikingly is purpose-built for single-page websites. It's ideal for landing pages, event pages, product launches, and personal portfolios where all content lives on one scrollable page.

**Best For:**
- Landing pages and product launches
- Event and conference pages
- Simple personal portfolios and resumes

**Strengths:**
- Best-in-class single-page site builder
- Simple, fast setup with no technical knowledge required
- Mobile-first responsive design out of the box
- Free tier allows multiple single-page sites

**Limitations:**
- Not suited for multi-page, content-heavy websites
- E-commerce functionality is basic compared to Shopify or Wix

---

### 10. Duda
**Website:** https://www.duda.co/
**Starting Price:** ~$19/month (Basic plan, annual)

**Overview:**
Duda is built for web design agencies and SaaS companies that need to build and manage websites at scale. Its white-label capabilities, client management tools, and team collaboration features make it the most agency-focused builder on this list.

**Best For:**
- Web design agencies managing multiple client sites
- SaaS companies that white-label website building for their customers
- Teams that need multi-user access and client portals

**Strengths:**
- White-label platform (your branding, not Duda's)
- Client management dashboard and permissions
- Widget Builder for custom functionality without code
- Multilingual site support built in

**Limitations:**
- Not designed for individual users or small solo sites
- Higher price point than consumer builders
