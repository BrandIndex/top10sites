## Detailed Software Profiles

### 1. QuickBooks Online
**Website:** https://quickbooks.intuit.com/
**Starting Price:** ~$17.50/month (Simple Start, promotional first 3 months)

**Overview:**
QuickBooks Online is the undisputed market leader in small business accounting software in the United States, with over 7 million subscribers. Nearly every US accountant and bookkeeper knows it, making it the safest choice for businesses that work with external CPAs or bookkeepers.

**Best For:**
- US-based small businesses that need their accountant to have access
- Growing businesses with inventory, payroll, and multi-user needs
- Companies that want the largest ecosystem of third-party integrations

**Strengths:**
- 750+ app integrations (Shopify, PayPal, HubSpot, Square, etc.)
- Payroll add-on available natively (QuickBooks Payroll)
- Inventory management on Plus and Advanced plans
- Mileage tracking, receipt capture, and time tracking built in
- Every US accountant knows QBO — easy to hand off

**Limitations:**
- Renewal prices are high (promotional rates expire)
- UI has improved but still lags behind FreshBooks for usability
- Live chat support quality is inconsistent

### 2. Xero
**Website:** https://www.xero.com/
**Starting Price:** ~$15/month (Starter plan, US pricing)

**Overview:**
Xero is the leading cloud accounting platform outside the US, dominant in Australia, New Zealand, the UK, and Southeast Asia. Its clean, modern interface, unlimited user seats on all plans, and 1,000+ integrations make it a serious QuickBooks alternative globally.

**Best For:**
- International businesses outside the US
- Teams that need unlimited users without per-seat costs
- Businesses that want a cleaner interface than QuickBooks

**Strengths:**
- Unlimited users on all plans (no per-seat user fees)
- 1,000+ integrations including Stripe, HubSpot, and inventory tools
- Bank reconciliation with smart matching
- Xero Expenses and Projects on higher plans
- Multi-currency support (business plan and above)

**Limitations:**
- US payroll integration is third-party (Gusto), not native
- Starter plan has invoice and bill limits
- Less accountant adoption in the US than QuickBooks

---

### 3. FreshBooks
**Website:** https://www.freshbooks.com/
**Starting Price:** ~$7.60/month (Lite, promotional first 4 months)

**Overview:**
FreshBooks is the easiest-to-use accounting software on this list, specifically designed for service-based freelancers and small businesses. Its invoicing, time tracking, and project management features are stronger than any pure accounting tool, and the interface is the most beautiful in the category.

**Best For:**
- Freelancers and independent contractors
- Service businesses (consultants, agencies, lawyers, designers)
- Users who find QuickBooks or Xero overwhelming

**Strengths:**
- Best-in-class invoicing with online payment acceptance
- Built-in time tracking and project profitability reports
- Proposal and contract creation tools
- Automatic expense categorization via bank feeds
- Beautiful, genuinely intuitive interface

**Limitations:**
- Lite plan limits billable clients (5 clients)
- Less suitable for product-based businesses needing inventory
- Fewer integrations than QuickBooks

---

### 4. Wave
**Website:** https://www.waveapps.com/
**Starting Price:** Free (core accounting) / ~$16/month (with payroll)

**Overview:**
Wave provides genuinely free accounting software — income and expense tracking, invoicing, receipt scanning, and financial reports — with no transaction limits or time expiry. It monetizes through optional payroll, payment processing, and advisory services.

**Best For:**
- Freelancers, sole proprietors, and micro-businesses on a zero budget
- Small businesses that want basic accounting without any monthly fee
- Businesses with simple financials (no inventory, no multi-currency)

**Strengths:**
- Core accounting entirely free — no expiry, no transaction limits
- Unlimited income/expense tracking and invoicing
- Free mobile receipt scanning
- Wave Money (business banking) available in the US

**Limitations:**
- Customer support is paid (Wave Pro subscription)
- Limited integrations compared to QuickBooks or Xero
- Not suitable for businesses needing inventory or payroll complexity

---

### 5. Zoho Books
**Website:** https://www.zoho.com/books/
**Starting Price:** Free (up to $50K/year revenue) / ~$15/month (Standard)

**Overview:**
Zoho Books is a full-featured accounting platform that integrates natively with Zoho's suite of business apps (CRM, Inventory, Projects, Analytics). For businesses already in the Zoho ecosystem, it's the natural accounting layer. Its free plan (for businesses under $50K annual revenue) is the most generous of any major accounting tool.

**Best For:**
- Businesses using Zoho CRM, Inventory, or other Zoho products
- Small businesses under $50K revenue who want a free full-featured option
- Businesses needing multi-currency or GST/VAT compliance (India, UAE, etc.)

**Strengths:**
- Free plan for businesses under $50K annual revenue
- Deep integration with 45+ Zoho apps
- Strong GST, VAT, and global tax compliance
- Automated payment reminders and recurring billing
- Client portal for sharing invoices and statements

**Limitations:**
- Less accountant adoption than QuickBooks, especially in the US
- Free plan limited to one user

### 6. Sage 50
**Website:** https://www.sage.com/en-us/products/sage-50/
**Starting Price:** ~$58.92/month (Pro Accounting, annual billing)

**Overview:**
Sage 50 (formerly Peachtree) is a desktop-first accounting platform with decades of history. While cloud syncing is now available, its core architecture is local — which appeals to businesses that want their data on their own machines and don't rely on internet connectivity.

**Best For:**
- Established businesses that prefer desktop software
- Manufacturing and distribution businesses needing advanced inventory
- US businesses with complex job costing needs

**Strengths:**
- Advanced inventory management (FIFO, LIFO, average cost)
- Job costing and project tracking built in
- Audit trail and tight user permissions for compliance
- Works offline (no internet dependency for core features)

**Limitations:**
- Most expensive entry point on this list
- Desktop-centric design feels dated next to cloud-native competitors
- Collaboration and remote access less seamless than Xero or QBO

### 7. FreeAgent
**Website:** https://www.freeagent.com/
**Starting Price:** ~$12/month (annual billing, UK pricing)

**Overview:**
FreeAgent is purpose-built for UK freelancers and small businesses, with native support for UK Self Assessment tax returns, VAT returns, and Making Tax Digital (MTD) compliance. NatWest and Royal Bank of Scotland customers get FreeAgent free as part of their business banking.

**Best For:**
- UK-based freelancers and sole traders
- NatWest or RBS business banking customers (free access included)
- UK small businesses needing MTD VAT compliance

**Strengths:**
- Full UK Self Assessment and VAT return filing within the app
- Making Tax Digital (MTD) compliant
- Free for NatWest and RBS business banking customers
- Time tracking, project management, and expense management built in
- Clear tax timeline showing upcoming tax deadlines

**Limitations:**
- Primarily UK-focused (limited value outside the UK)
- Fewer integrations than QuickBooks or Xero

### 8. Bench
**Website:** https://bench.co/
**Starting Price:** ~$299/month (Essential, monthly billing)

**Overview:**
Bench is not traditional accounting software — it's a managed bookkeeping service backed by software. Real human bookkeepers handle your monthly bookkeeping, powered by Bench's proprietary platform. For businesses that don't want to do their own books, Bench handles everything and delivers clean monthly financial statements.

**Best For:**
- Business owners who hate bookkeeping and want to outsource it entirely
- Businesses that need clean books for tax filing without DIY effort
- Startups and SMBs that can't justify a full-time bookkeeper

**Strengths:**
- Real human bookkeepers do your books every month
- Clean, intuitive dashboard for financial reporting
- Tax preparation available as an add-on (Bench Tax)
- Catch-up bookkeeping for businesses behind on their books

**Limitations:**
- Significantly more expensive than DIY software
- Less real-time — books updated monthly, not daily
- Less suitable for businesses with complex inventory or job costing

---

### 9. Kashoo
**Website:** https://kashoo.com/
**Starting Price:** ~$20/month (Kashoo plan, annual billing)

**Overview:**
Kashoo is the simplest true double-entry cloud accounting software available. Its AI-powered transaction categorization, minimalist interface, and straightforward feature set make it ideal for small business owners who want accurate books without spending time learning software.

**Best For:**
- Small business owners who want simple, clean accounting
- Businesses transitioning from spreadsheets
- Users who find QuickBooks or Xero too complex

**Strengths:**
- Extremely simple interface — minimal learning curve
- AI-powered auto-categorization of bank transactions
- True double-entry accounting (proper accrual accounting)
- Unlimited users included at no extra cost

**Limitations:**
- Limited advanced features (no inventory, no payroll, no job costing)
- Smaller integration ecosystem
- Less suitable for growing businesses with complex needs

---

### 10. GnuCash
**Website:** https://www.gnucash.org/
**Starting Price:** Free (open-source)

**Overview:**
GnuCash is a free, open-source, double-entry accounting application available for desktop (Windows, Mac, Linux). It supports multi-currency transactions, stock/mutual fund tracking, and has the full power of a professional accounting system — at zero cost.

**Best For:**
- Tech-savvy individuals and small businesses wanting free accounting
- Users who want full data control on their own machine
- Developers and open-source advocates

**Strengths:**
- Completely free — no subscriptions, no limits
- Full double-entry accounting with reports
- Stock and mutual fund portfolio tracking
- Multi-currency support
- Runs entirely locally — no internet dependency

**Limitations:**
- Dated desktop interface with a steep learning curve
- No cloud sync or mobile apps
- No bank feeds (manual import required)
