## Detailed Hosting Profiles

---

### 1. SiteGround
**Website:** https://www.siteground.com/
**Starting Price:** ~$2.99/month (StartUp plan, promotional)

**Overview:**
SiteGround is widely regarded as the best shared hosting provider for WordPress sites. Its proprietary SuperCacher and in-house CDN consistently deliver load times well below industry average, and its 24/7 live chat support is among the most responsive in the business.

**Best For:**
- WordPress blogs and business sites
- Developers who value fast, knowledgeable support
- Sites needing reliable uptime (99.99% SLA)

**Strengths:**
- Proprietary SuperCacher and SG Optimizer for WordPress speed
- Free daily backups (30-day retention on higher plans)
- Built-in CDN and free SSL
- Excellent 24/7 live chat support with WordPress expertise

**Limitations:**
- Renewal rates are significantly higher than promotional pricing
- Storage limits are tighter than some competitors at the base tier

---

### 2. Bluehost
**Website:** https://www.bluehost.com/
**Starting Price:** ~$2.95/month (Basic plan, promotional)

**Overview:**
Bluehost is one of the original WordPress-recommended hosts and remains the most popular choice for WordPress beginners. Its one-click WordPress installation, generous storage, and free domain (first year) make it a complete starter package.

**Best For:**
- First-time website owners
- WordPress bloggers and small business sites
- Users who want an all-in-one package (domain + hosting + WordPress)

**Strengths:**
- Official WordPress.org recommended host
- Free domain name for the first year
- Free SSL certificate included
- Intuitive cPanel-based control panel

**Limitations:**
- Shared hosting performance can slow under heavy traffic
- Upsells are aggressive during checkout

---

### 3. Hostinger
**Website:** https://www.hostinger.com/
**Starting Price:** ~$1.99/month (Single plan, promotional)

**Overview:**
Hostinger delivers impressive performance at the lowest price point in the industry. Its proprietary hPanel control panel is more modern than cPanel, and its LiteSpeed servers give it a speed edge over similarly priced competitors.

**Best For:**
- Budget-constrained users who still want quality
- Beginners migrating from expensive hosts
- Developers building multiple sites

**Strengths:**
- Best price-to-performance ratio in shared hosting
- LiteSpeed servers and free Cloudflare CDN
- Modern hPanel (custom control panel)
- 100+ one-click installers

**Limitations:**
- No free domain on the cheapest plan
- Phone support not available

---

### 4. A2 Hosting
**Website:** https://www.a2hosting.com/
**Starting Price:** ~$2.99/month (Startup plan, promotional)

**Overview:**
A2 Hosting markets itself as the "Turbo Hosting" provider and delivers on that claim. Its Turbo plans use LiteSpeed servers with 20× faster speeds versus standard Apache, making it a developer favorite for PHP applications and high-performance WordPress sites.

**Best For:**
- Speed-obsessed developers and WordPress power users
- PHP applications needing high throughput
- Users who want developer-friendly tools (SSH, Git, WP-CLI)

**Strengths:**
- Turbo plans with LiteSpeed servers (20× speed claim)
- Anytime money-back guarantee (even after 30 days, prorated)
- Developer-friendly: SSH, Git, Drush, WP-CLI supported
- Free site migrations

**Limitations:**
- Turbo performance requires a more expensive plan tier
- Support quality can vary

---

### 5. DreamHost
**Website:** https://www.dreamhost.com/
**Starting Price:** ~$2.59/month (Shared Starter, annual billing)

**Overview:**
DreamHost is one of the oldest independent web hosts (founded 1997) and is the only host besides SiteGround and Bluehost to receive official WordPress.org recommendation. It offers a unique 97-day money-back guarantee and strong privacy protections.

**Best For:**
- Users who want a reliable, long-established independent host
- WordPress developers who value a clean, modern custom panel
- Privacy-focused users (DreamHost does not resell user data)

**Strengths:**
- 97-day money-back guarantee — the longest in the industry
- Official WordPress.org recommended host
- Custom DreamPanel (no cPanel upsell)
- Free WHOIS privacy included

**Limitations:**
- Live chat available only during business hours
- Shared Starter plan limits to one website

### 6. InMotion Hosting
**Website:** https://www.inmotionhosting.com/
**Starting Price:** ~$2.99/month (Core plan, promotional)

**Overview:**
InMotion Hosting focuses on small business and professional websites with a strong emphasis on US-based customer support. It offers free business email accounts and an automatic website builder tool, making it a solid all-in-one for SMBs.

**Best For:**
- Small business owners who need hosting + business email
- WooCommerce and e-commerce sites
- Users who want US-based 24/7 phone and chat support

**Strengths:**
- Free business email hosting included
- US-based 24/7 phone, chat, and email support
- Free website builder and one-click app installer
- SSD storage on all plans

**Limitations:**
- Primarily US-focused data centers
- Renewal rates climb noticeably

### 7. GreenGeeks
**Website:** https://www.greengeeks.com/
**Starting Price:** ~$2.95/month (Lite plan, promotional)

**Overview:**
GreenGeeks is the leading eco-friendly web host. For every unit of energy it uses, it returns 300% back to the grid via renewable energy credits. Performance is competitive, and it's a great choice for sustainability-conscious businesses.

**Best For:**
- Brands that want to highlight sustainability credentials
- Small to medium WordPress sites
- Users who want solid performance + green credentials

**Strengths:**
- 300% renewable energy match (certified green host)
- Free CDN (Cloudflare), SSL, and nightly backups
- LiteSpeed servers with caching
- 30-day money-back guarantee

**Limitations:**
- Premium pricing relative to Hostinger for similar performance
- Only 3 US-based data centers

### 8. HostGator
**Website:** https://www.hostgator.com/
**Starting Price:** ~$2.75/month (Hatchling plan, promotional)

**Overview:**
HostGator is one of the largest shared hosting providers by customer volume. It's a dependable workhorse for high-traffic shared hosting and supports unlimited domains on higher plans, making it practical for agencies managing multiple client sites.

**Best For:**
- Agencies hosting multiple client websites
- High-traffic shared hosting on a budget
- Users who want unlimited bandwidth

**Strengths:**
- Unlimited disk space and bandwidth on most plans
- 45-day money-back guarantee
- 24/7 phone, live chat, and email support
- Free domain for the first year

**Limitations:**
- Shared hosting performance has declined relative to LiteSpeed-based competitors
- Aggressive upsells at checkout

---

### 9. WP Engine
**Website:** https://wpengine.com/
**Starting Price:** ~$20/month (Startup plan)

**Overview:**
WP Engine is the leading managed WordPress hosting platform for professional sites and agencies. Everything is tuned specifically for WordPress — caching, security, staging environments, and developer tools — at a premium price point.

**Best For:**
- Professional WordPress developers and agencies
- Business sites that cannot tolerate downtime
- Teams that need staging environments for testing

**Strengths:**
- Purpose-built for WordPress (proprietary EverCache technology)
- One-click staging environments and easy deployment
- Automatic daily backups with one-click restore
- Global CDN included

**Limitations:**
- Significantly more expensive than shared hosting
- WordPress-only (no Joomla, Drupal, etc.)
- Traffic-based pricing can be limiting

---

### 10. Kinsta
**Website:** https://kinsta.com/
**Starting Price:** ~$35/month (Starter plan)

**Overview:**
Kinsta runs entirely on Google Cloud Platform (Premium Tier) and is positioned as the elite choice for performance-critical WordPress sites. With data centers in 37 locations and cutting-edge infrastructure, Kinsta is the go-to for enterprise-grade WordPress hosting.

**Best For:**
- High-traffic WordPress sites needing maximum performance
- Enterprise teams who want Google Cloud infrastructure
- Developers who want a premium DevOps-grade dashboard

**Strengths:**
- Google Cloud Platform Premium Tier infrastructure
- 37 data center locations worldwide
- Application, database, and WordPress hosting from one dashboard
- Excellent MyKinsta dashboard with analytics, staging, and redirects

**Limitations:**
- Most expensive option in this list
- WordPress-only managed hosting
